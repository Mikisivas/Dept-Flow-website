Component 01 · the signature

CheckpointStrip

A lecture is scored out of two checkpoints, so a session draws as two cells. Filled means captured, hollow means missed, dashed means recorded but not yet counted. This is the one place the design spends its boldness.

Rules

  • Every pair carries a text label on its wrapper — the motif is never the only carrier of meaning.
  • Missed cells use #767676 (4.54:1) and provisional #949494 (3.03:1) — both clear the 3:1 boundary rule, because the border is the only carrier of missed-vs-captured.
  • The fill animation honours prefers-reduced-motion: the cells snap instead.

Component 02

StatusBadge

Icon, label and colour together — never colour alone. Five variants, matching the five compliance states exactly. Provisional is the only one with no fill, because it carries no judgement.

Counted Not yet counted Checking payment… Attendance locked At risk

Why locked fills and at-risk outlines

Both are red. Locked is a fact the system has enforced, so it is solid. At risk is advisory — a prediction, not a determination — so it is outlined and reads quieter at the same colour.

Component 04

AppShell

Students get a bottom tab bar because they hold a phone one-handed. HOD and admin get a desktop sidebar. The header carries the simplified mark, never the full crest.

Rules

  • The active tab is marked by a 2px orange rule and a weight change — never colour alone.
  • Bottom bar respects env(safe-area-inset-bottom); every target is at least 44×44.
  • Nav differs per role by omission, not by disabled items: the HOD sidebar has no configuration entry at all.
  • A skip link precedes the header on every page.

Component 08

ConfirmDialog

Authority actions state exactly what will happen and how many records move, then require a typed reason. The reason field is the audit log's entry point, so it is never optional.

Rules

  • The confirm button repeats the action's own name — never "OK" or "Confirm".
  • Destructive variants swap the fill to #B91C1C with white text; an orange fill would read as routine.
  • On mobile this becomes a bottom sheet with overscroll-behavior: contain.
  • Radix Dialog supplies the focus trap and escape handling — not hand-written.
