Notes

  • The preview panel is live — switch the scope radio above and the count changes before you commit. A number that only appears in the dialog gets skimmed.
  • Grace unlocks recording; it never counts sessions. Both the form and the dialog say so, because conflating them would be the expensive misunderstanding.
  • The confirm button repeats the action's name, and the cancel is "Back to the form" — nobody loses their typed reason.

Waivers and disputes

Both queues state the effect of the decision in sessions, not in abstractions — a waiver that counts nine sessions is a different decision from one that counts two.

/hod/eligibility · /hod/lecturers

Eligibility list and lecturer oversight

The eligibility list is the final output of the whole system, and authorizing it is a signature, not an export. Nothing prints until it is signed.

Notes

  • Print is disabled until the list is authorized — an unsigned list circulating as a PDF is the failure mode this page exists to prevent.
  • The provisional warning sits above the table, not in the dialog, because it is a reason not to press the button at all.
  • Lecturer oversight is comparative by design: one lecturer's 38% only means something beside a colleague's 8%.
  • The flagged lecturer row is tinted and carries a red figure with a session count, so it survives a greyscale print.
