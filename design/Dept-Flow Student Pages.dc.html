Everything here is 390px wide because students only use phones. Code entry gets the most room — it runs in a noisy hall against a countdown, and each of its six rejections has to say something different.

Route

/dashboard student · 4 states

Dashboard

One question: am I on track. The compliance banner comes before any greeting, because a locked student who scrolls past it loses a session.

Notes

  • Every state answers "what would clearing get me" with a real number — 38% → 76%. An abstract "not counted" doesn't move anyone to pay.
  • The locked banner still tells them to attend class, because the lecturer's register and a later waiver can recover the session. A lock that reads as "don't bother" costs them the term.
  • Locked is the only screen in the product with a full-bleed red field — it is the one state where the page itself should feel stopped.
  • Per-course cards carry the strip, not a second meter: the pattern of halves is the diagnosis, a percentage is only the symptom.

Notifications and profile

Profile shows what the department holds and nothing more. There is no location history here, because there is no location history in the system.

Carried through this group

  • No coordinates, anywhere. Profile has no location section at all — not an empty one. The privacy promise is kept by absence, not by a setting.
  • Provisional never turns orange. Dashed outline, muted text, no fill — it's a waiting state, not a warning.
  • Every orange surface carries black text. The keypad's submit, the dues button, the unread dot's border — all at 9.30:1.
  • Read-only identity. Name, matric and level come from the register; programme is derived from the matric code and shown, never asked.
